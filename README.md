> **TRIPORA** is a comprehensive planetary encyclopedia and sovereign travel atlas covering all **195 United Nations Member States**, major world metropolises, historical milestones, visa guidelines, daily travel budgets, and essential cultural secrets.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🌍 195 Sovereign UN Nations Atlas
- **Complete Global Coverage**: Comprehensive dossiers for all 195 UN-recognized sovereign countries across all 7 continents.
- **Detailed Country Profiles**:
  - **Vital Statistics**: Capital, population, ISO codes, currencies, driving orientation, electrical plug types & voltage, and international calling codes.
  - **Visa & Entry Regulations**: Up-to-date entry rules, standard visa validity, and requirements.
  - **Safety & Emergency Data**: National safety ratings (0–100), travel advisories, and local emergency phone numbers (Police, Ambulance, Fire).
  - **Budget Calculations**: Daily expense tiers categorized for Budget ($), Mid-Range ($$), and Luxury ($$$) travelers.
  - **Culture, Etiquette & Taboos**: Crucial dos and don'ts, social norms, and traditional annual festivals.
  - **Historical Timelines**: Multi-era historical milestones from ancient origins to modern sovereignty.

### 2. 🏙️ World Metropolises & Capitals Explorer
- **Deep-Dive City Guides**: In-depth coverage of world capitals and major cultural centers (e.g., Tokyo, Paris, Rome, London, New York, Kolkata, New Delhi, Mumbai, Bangkok, Singapore, Dubai, Seoul, and more).
- **Urban Details**: Must-visit landmarks, historic roots, local gastronomic dishes, cultural highlights, and day trips.

### 3. 🧰 Travel Planning & Decision Toolkits
- **Interactive Multi-Currency Budget Calculator**: Estimate trip expenses with live conversion support for USD ($), EUR (€), INR (₹), GBP (£), and JPY (¥).
- **Smart Packing Checklist Generator**: Automated gear recommendations based on destination climate, planned activities, and trip duration.
- **Visa Prerequisite Finder**: Fast reference tool to verify entry requirements before traveling.
- **Essential Traveler FAQs**: Guidance on international SIMs/eSIMs, travel insurance, cash exchange, tipping culture, and emergency prep.

### 4. 🔍 Search, Bookmarks & Navigation
- **Global Search (`⌘K`)**: Instant search across 195 nations, cities, cultural wonders, and culinary specialties.
- **Personal Bucket List**: Client-side bookmarking to save favorite destinations and itineraries.
- **Chapter Scroll Spy**: Smooth navigation header with active section tracking.
